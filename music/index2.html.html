Artist

For Ameen, music begins with emotion.

A.R. Ameen represents a new generation of global creatives moving fluidly between music, entrepreneurship, culture, and innovation. His journey is not simply about following a path. It is about building one.

His artistic journey has taken him from Indian cinema and live stages around the world to a new chapter centered on independent music, original collaborations, and a sound increasingly his own.

Ameen first reached audiences through songs including Maula Wa Sallim, followed by performances at sold-out venues globally, and recordings across multiple Indian languages. His work has continued to evolve through film music, independent releases, and collaborations with artists and creators from different musical worlds. His 2026 release Bheegi Bheegi marked another step in that evolution and the beginning of a new chapter through Rooh Records.

Rather than defining himself through one genre, language, or market, Ameen approaches music with curiosity.

Indian musical traditions can meet contemporary pop.Cinematic storytelling can meet independent music.Sounds from different cultures can exist within the same creative world.

A global perspective

Ameen's ambition is to create music that travels beyond geography. That means collaborating across cultures, experimenting with new genres, and bringing together artists whose musical identities might otherwise never intersect.

Create music that feels personal enough to be authentic and universal enough to connect.

A venture founded by A.R. Ameen

Rooh Records

“Rooh” means soul.

That philosophy led to the creation of Rooh Records: a home where music can be developed with patience, authenticity, and creative freedom.

It was conceived as a platform for new sounds, new voices, and new-age storytelling, combining emotional depth with contemporary production and an international outlook. Building Rooh is not about chasing short-term attention. It is about developing something with the strength and identity to endure.

Beyond the record label

Rooh represents the beginning of a broader entrepreneurial journey. Ameen is interested in businesses and partnerships that can reshape how creativity is developed, experienced, and shared.

  • Developing original music, catalogs, and long-term creative assets.

  • Creating opportunities for emerging artists, producers, writers, and collaborators.

  • Exploring how emerging technologies can expand music creation, distribution and fan experiences.

  • Building experiences that connect audiences with music beyond traditional recordings.

  • Creating bridges between India and creative communities worldwide.

  • Developing ideas where music, design, media, and contemporary culture intersect: technology, investments, fashion, wellness, film, philanthropy.

A world built around sound.

The next generation of creative companies will not fit neatly into one category.

Neither will the people building them.

Ameen's entrepreneurial vision is to build ventures where creativity and commerce are strengthened by one another, without compromising the soul of the music.
